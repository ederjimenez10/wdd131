Caracas, Venezuela

Venezuela is the 33rd largest country in the world and is located on the northern coast of South America, where the Andes Mountains meet the Caribbean Sea. It is home to roughly 7% of the planet’s plant and animal species, ranking it as one of the world's 17 "megadiverse" nations. The country is best known for Angel Falls—the world’s tallest waterfall—its massive oil reserves, and the highest concentration of lightning strikes ever recorded by Guinness.
